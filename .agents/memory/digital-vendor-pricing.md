---
name: Digital vendor pricing
description: Base and variation price responsibilities for vendor digital products.
---

For digital-only vendor products, the vendor-level unit price stays at 0; pricing is entered on configured variation options. Cloud services and subscriptions are digital products. Hybrid products retain their physical workflow.

**Why:** The user specified that option variations own digital pricing because a nonzero base price duplicates those amounts.

**How to apply:** Keep digital vendor base prices at 0 across add, edit, import, save, and display flows. Preserve variation price adjustments, and keep Pricing Model and Tenure/Billing hidden from vendor digital listing and add/edit pages.

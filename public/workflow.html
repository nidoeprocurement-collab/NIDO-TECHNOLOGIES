Shop → Purchase Order Process Flow

End-to-end workflow: from a client browsing the product catalog to a Purchase Order being raised with a vendor.

1 · Shop
STEP 1
Browse Product Catalog
Client visits the Shop page. Products are filtered by their client-specific catalog assignment and displayed with live stock availability.
ShopPage.tsxclientCatalogStore
STEP 1 — SYSTEM
Load Client Catalog
getClientCatalog(clientId) fetches the product list assigned to this client. Live stock = physical stock − reserved stock per vendor.
clientCatalogStore.tsvendorStore.ts
STEP 2
Add Product to Cart
Client clicks "Add to Cart" from the product card or ProductDetailDialog, choosing quantity.
CartContext.addItem()
STEP 2 — SYSTEM
Immediate Stock Reservation
stockEngine.reserveExtra() is called instantly. The requested qty is locked against a specific vendor allocation — preventing overselling before checkout.
stockEngine.tsvendorAllocations
2 · Cart
STEP 3
Review Cart
Client reviews items, adjusts quantities. Page shows enriched pricing: GST rates, HSN codes, volume-slab discounts applied automatically.
CartPage.tsx
STEP 3 — SYSTEM
Tax & Price Engine
Each line item is enriched with HSN codes + GST (SGST/CGST) via taxEngine.ts and volume-based slab discounts via priceEngineStore.ts.
taxEngine.tspriceEngineStore.ts
3 · Checkout
STEP 4
Enter Delivery Address
Client selects or enters a delivery address. Addresses are saved per-client in localStorage for reuse.
CheckoutPage.tsx
STEP 5
Select Payment Method
Client chooses payment terms (e.g. Net-30, advance). Selection is persisted as nido_payment_selection in localStorage.
PaymentPage.tsx
4 · Order
STEP 6
Review & Place Order
Client reviews the full order summary on ReviewPage and clicks "Place Order".
ReviewPage.tsx
STEP 6 — SYSTEM
Master Order Created
MyOrdersContext.addOrder() creates a master order record with auto-incremented ID (e.g. ORD-000001), status = "Processing". Cart is cleared via consumeCart().
MyOrdersContext.tsxstatus: Processing
STEP 7 — SYSTEM
Vendor Auto-Assignment
Price engine attempts to auto-select the optimal vendor per line item based on pricing rules, availability, and contract terms already configured.
priceEngineStore.tsvendorStore.ts
STEP 7 — ADMIN
Order Appears in Admin View
Order appears in the procurement admin's order list as "Processing". Admin opens OrderDetailsPage to review line items and vendor assignments.
OrderDetailsPage.tsx
5 · PO Raise
STEP 8
Confirm Vendor Selection
Admin verifies or manually overrides vendor for each line item. Confirms pricing, delivery date, and payment terms before proceeding.
OrderDetailsPage.tsx
STEP 9 — SYSTEM
PO Generated & PDF Created
generatePoNumber() creates a unique PO number (e.g. PO-00009). PO record saved to purchaseOrderStore. PDF generated via generatePO.ts. Stock moves from Reserved → Physical Deduction.
purchaseOrderStore.tsgeneratePO.tsstock deducted
STEP 9
Click "Raise Purchase Orders"
Admin clicks the "Raise Purchase Orders" button. One PO is generated per vendor involved in the order. PO status = "Confirmed". PDF available for download/send.
Raise PO buttonstatus: Confirmed

 Key Data at Each Stage 

🛍️
Cart Item
Data captured when added to cart
  • productId, productName — catalog identifier
  • vendorId — auto-assigned vendor from price engine
  • qty — quantity requested
  • unitPrice — base price (before GST)
  • reservationId — stock lock reference from stockEngine
🧾
Cart Page Enrichment
Tax & discount layer applied at CartPage
  • hsnCode — from taxEngine per product category
  • gstRate — total GST % (SGST + CGST split)
  • slabDiscount — volume discount from priceEngineStore
  • lineTotal — qty × unitPrice − discount + tax
📋
Master Order (ORD-XXXXXX)
Created at Place Order
  • orderId — auto-incremented e.g. ORD-000001
  • clientId, clientName
  • deliveryAddress — from CheckoutPage
  • paymentTerms — from PaymentPage
  • lineItems[] — enriched cart items
  • status — "Processing"
  • createdAt — timestamp
📄
Purchase Order (PO-XXXXX)
Generated at Raise PO step
  • poNumber — e.g. PO-00009
  • orderId — links back to master order
  • vendorId, vendorName, GSTIN — vendor details
  • vendorAddress, vendorContact
  • lineItems[] — sku, productCode, hsnCode, qty, unitPrice
  • subtotal, sgstAmt, cgstAmt, grandTotal
  • requestedDelivery, paymentTerms
  • status — "Confirmed"

 Actor Responsibilities 

🧑‍💼
Client / End User
Steps 1 – 6
  • Browse the shop and select products
  • Adjust quantities in cart
  • Provide delivery address and payment preference
  • Review and confirm the order
  • No further action needed — procurement team handles the rest
👔
Procurement Admin
Steps 7 – 9
  • Monitor incoming orders in the Orders queue
  • Open OrderDetailsPage for each Processing order
  • Verify or override auto-assigned vendor per line item
  • Confirm delivery date and payment terms
  • Click "Raise Purchase Orders" to generate POs
  • Download / send PO PDF to vendor
⚙️
System (Automated)
All phases
  • Reserves stock the moment an item is added to cart
  • Applies HSN codes, GST rates, and slab discounts automatically
  • Attempts auto-vendor selection via price engine
  • Generates PO number, saves record, produces PDF
  • Moves stock from Reserved → Physically Deducted on PO raise
